The clipboard history for Mac.

Copy anything. Paste any of your last ten.

Your clipboard, upgraded

Every copy, kept

Graffiti remembers your last ten clipboard copies: text, code, screenshots, images and files, each with a preview.

One key

⌃⌘V opens the wall right where you are typing. Pick a number and it pastes.

Clean paste

Hold ⇧ to paste plain text with straight quotes and no prompts, ready for a terminal.

Secrets stay secret

Passwords and keys show as dots, are never saved, and clear themselves after a minute.

Install

  1. Open Graffiti.dmg and drag Graffiti to Applications.
  2. Open Graffiti from Applications. If your Mac blocks it, click Done.
  3. Double-click Allow Graffiti in the installer window, scroll down, click Open Anyway, then Open.
  4. Choose Allow Pasting and Open at Login, and switch Graffiti on in the settings that open. Look for the spray can in your menu bar. Updates arrive by themselves.

How to use it

⌃⌘VOpen the wall wherever you are typing
1 – 9, 0Paste that clip, or click it
⇧ + numberPaste it clean, as plain text
HPaste your held clip. Hold one with the pin, or ⇧H
↑ ↓ returnChoose and paste
escClose the wall

Free, and private

Graffiti is free. No account, no sign-up, no ads.

It keeps your wall on your Mac only, in memory while it runs. Your clips are never sent anywhere and are not stored in any database but your own Mac. Passwords and keys are never saved and clear themselves after a minute. When Graffiti updates itself, it saves your wall on your Mac for a moment so it survives the restart, then deletes that copy.

The only time Graffiti goes online is to check for updates, once a day. That check sends none of your clips. We count downloads, installs and daily update checks as plain totals, with the version number. No names, no addresses, no IDs.
