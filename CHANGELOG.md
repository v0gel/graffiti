# Changelog

Newest first. Versions before 0.1.3 weren't released.

## 0.2.7 (2026-10-09)
- Allow Pasting stays in the menu now, with a check mark once pasting is allowed. Click it to
  jump to the Accessibility settings.

## 0.2.6 (2026-10-09)
- Counts installs and one update check a day, as plain totals with the version.
- The installer shows its version in its name and window.

## 0.2.5 (2026-10-09)
- New look. App icon, menu bar can and wordmark, all hand drawn.
- The installer walks you through first-time setup in three steps, with a shortcut to the
  security settings.

## 0.2.4 (2026-10-08)
- Pin button on every clip to hold it.
- The held clip shows at the top of the menu, with Hold a Clip and Clear Held Clip.

## 0.2.3 (2026-10-08)
- When there's an update the menu says New Update, and opens what changed with Install and
  Restart.

## 0.2.2 (2026-10-08)
- Your wall survives updates.
- Copies from your iPhone or iPad (Universal Clipboard) come in as text only. Reading them in
  full was slowing Handoff down.

## 0.2.1 (2026-10-08)
- Fixed updates being refused. Copies on 0.2.0 or older have to be installed by hand once.

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-08)
- Hold one named clip outside the ten. H pastes it, ⇧H holds the selected one.
- Agents can read the wall with the `graffiti` command, if you turn it on.

## 0.1.9 (2026-10-08)
- The list is called the wall now.
- About window, and dialogs that look the same on every Mac.

## 0.1.8 (2026-10-08)
- Signed updates. Check for Updates in the menu.

## 0.1.7 (2026-10-08)
- Pasting keeps the list in order. Last Pasted Clip to Top in the menu if you want the old way.

## 0.1.6 (2026-10-07)
- Pictures copied from Apple Notes come through, full size.

## 0.1.5 (2026-10-07)
- Pictures paste into Claude Code and terminals as attachments. Text with pictures pastes the
  text, then each picture.

## 0.1.4 (2026-10-07)
- Keeps the whole copy, so text with images pastes intact.
- Thumbnails for image files.
- Pasting permission sticks between versions.

## 0.1.3 (2026-10-07)
- Images win over the text attached to them.
- The wall stays open until you pick something.
- Version number in the menu.

## Before that (2026-10-07)
- ⌃⌘V wall with previews, clean paste, the secret guard, universal build for Apple silicon and
  Intel, and the installer.
